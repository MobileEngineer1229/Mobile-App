### BabyCenter
- **Focus**: Pregnancy tracking → Baby's first year
- **Target Audience**: Expecting parents and new parents (0-12 months)
- **Business Model**: Free with ads, premium features
- **Platform**: iOS, Android

- **Platform**: iOS, Android
- **Rating**: High ratings (exact number varies)

## 🎯 FINAL FEATURE LIST - WHAT WE'RE ADDING

### ✅ CORE FEATURES (MVP - Launch Ready)

#### 1. Multiple Baby Profiles ⚠️ **MUST HAVE**
**What We're Adding**:
- Multiple baby profiles per user account
- Profile switching functionality
- Profile-specific data isolation
- Profile management (create, edit, delete, switch)

**Why**: Essential for families with multiple children. Both competitors support this.

---

#### 2. Daily Tracker (Feeding, Sleep, Diaper) ⚠️ **MUST HAVE**
**What We're Adding**:

**Feeding Tracker**:
- Breastfeeding sessions tracking
- Formula feeding tracking
- Solid food introduction
- Feeding schedule and history
- Feeding patterns analysis
- Feeding statistics/charts

**Sleep Tracker**:
- Sleep session logging (naps, bedtime)
- Sleep duration tracking
- Sleep patterns visualization
- Bedtime routine tracking
- Sleep statistics

**Diaper Tracker**:
- Wet/dirty diaper count
- Diaper change times
- Daily diaper summary
- Patterns analysis

**Why**: Essential daily tracking that parents use multiple times per day. Both BabyCenter and Kinedu have this.

---

#### 3. Video Content System ⚠️ **MUST HAVE**
**What We're Adding**:

**Video Upload & Storage**:
- Backend file upload service
- Cloud storage integration (AWS S3/Cloudinary)
- Video validation and processing
- Video compression
- Video thumbnail generation
- Mobile camera integration
- Gallery video picker

**Video Timeline & Player**:
- Video timeline view
- Video player component
- Video thumbnails
- Playback controls
- Fullscreen support

**Why**: 
- Kinedu has full video support (competitive requirement)
- BabyCenter doesn't have it (opportunity to beat them)
- Parents love capturing baby moments on video
- Essential for milestone and activity tracking

---

#### 4. Milestone via Video ⚠️ **MUST HAVE**
**What We're Adding**:
- Attach videos when achieving milestones
- Video playback in milestone detail
- Multiple videos per milestone
- Video gallery in milestone view

**Why**: Parents love capturing "first steps", "first words" on video. High user demand. Kinedu has this feature.

---

#### 5. Video to Activities ⭐ **UNIQUE - MUST HAVE**
**What We're Adding**:
- Attach videos to activity completion
- Video playback in activity detail
- Video-based talent assessment
- Activity completion video proof

**Why**: 
- **NO COMPETITOR HAS THIS!**
- Perfect for talent development tracking
- Parents can show baby's talent progress through videos
- Major differentiator for Talent Baby

---

#### 6. Personalized Daily Plan ⚠️ **MUST HAVE**
**What We're Adding**:

**Daily Activity Recommendations**:
- Algorithm for daily activity selection
- Age-based activity filtering
- Talent-based activity recommendations
- Daily plan generation
- Today's recommended activities
- Activity completion tracking
- Plan customization

**Daily Updates**:
- Age-appropriate tips
- Development information

**Why**:

- Highly personalized experience
- Keeps users coming back daily

---

#### 7. Basic Talent Assessments ⚠️ **MUST HAVE**
**What We're Adding** (Enhancing existing):
- Assessment questionnaire system
- Assessment scoring algorithm
- Assessment recommendations
- Assessment screens (one per talent category)
- Assessment questions UI
- Assessment results display
- Assessment history

**Why**: 
- Already partially implemented

- Core differentiator
- Needs enhancement for better UX

# Food Assistant Notes

For a healthy meal plan, the assistant should give concise and practical
answers. It should mention ingredients, preparation steps, and safety notes
when they are present in the documents.

Example guidance:

- Soup can be prepared by boiling broth first, then adding vegetables and
  protein.
- A balanced meal usually includes a protein source, vegetables, grains, and
  water.
- When food is prepared for children, avoid hard pieces and check for allergy
  risks.

# Must-Have Features for Talent Baby App

---

## 🎯 CRITICAL MUST-HAVE FEATURES (Implement First)

### 1. Video Upload & Storage ⚠️ **CRITICAL**
**Why**: 
- ✅ Kinedu has it (competitive requirement)
- ❌ BabyCenter doesn't (opportunity to beat them)
- ⭐ Unique opportunity for talent development videos

**What to Build**:
- Backend file upload service
- Cloud storage (AWS S3 or Cloudinary)
- Video upload UI in mobile app
- Video validation and processing

**Priority**: **P0 - CRITICAL**

### 2. Video Timeline & Player ⚠️ **CRITICAL**
**Why**:
- Parents need to see their videos

- Video timeline view
- Video player component
- Video thumbnails
- Playback controls

**Priority**: **P0 - CRITICAL**
